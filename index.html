About Akesi's Kreation

Our Origin Story

Akesi's Kreation began from a lifelong love of color, texture, and handmade art. Founded by multidisciplinary artist, occupational health and safety officer and chromotherapist Samuella Bennie Coffie, the brand celebrates the beauty of creativity through crochet fashion, wearable art, and mixed media paintings.

Samuella's journey started at just six years old, when she first discovered the calming rhythm of crochet and beading. Over time, her craft evolved into a way of expressing emotion and exploring the power of color to influence mood and well-being.

Every piece—from bold crochet earrings to fascinators, brooches, and wall art—reflects her belief that color is more than visual; it's therapeutic.

Meet the Maker

Samuella Bennie Coffie is the creative mind behind Akesi's Kreation—a multidisciplinary artist, chromotherapist, and lifelong lover of color.

Her journey with art began at age six, when she first picked up a crochet hook and discovered the peace that comes from creating with her hands.

Today, she combines her artistic background with her knowledge of chromotherapy to design pieces that do more than beautify—they uplift, calm, and inspire.

Samuella's work moves fluidly between crochet fashion, wearable art, and mixed media paintings. Through Akesi's Kreation, she invites people to experience color not just as decoration, but as a form of therapy and self-expression.

Our Journey

The Beginning

The Spark Ignites

A creative journey begins as our founder learns to bead and soon after, to crochet—laying the foundation for a lifelong passion for handcrafted art.

Brand Launch

Akesi's Kreation is Born

The vision becomes reality. Akesi's Kreation is officially launched, combining traditional techniques with a bold, contemporary aesthetic to create wearable art with meaning.
